INVEST529 DOCUMENTS  -  commands   (READ-ONLY)
============================================================
Run these from the PaperPull folder (paperpull.bat on Windows,
./paperpull on macOS). login.bat is the one double-click that stays.

  Task            You                                 Spouse
  --------------  ----------------------------------  ----------------------------------
  Sign in         login.bat                           login.bat spouse
  Look (no d/l)   paperpull invest529 diagnose      paperpull invest529 diagnose --account spouse
  Pilot (test)    paperpull invest529 pilot         paperpull invest529 pilot --account spouse
  Full run        paperpull invest529 all           paperpull invest529 all --account spouse
  Continue        paperpull invest529 resume        paperpull invest529 resume --account spouse
  Re-check PDFs   paperpull invest529 verify        paperpull invest529 verify --account spouse

FLOW:  login.bat  ->  paperpull invest529 pilot  ->  paperpull invest529 all
Sign in, open your Documents page, keep the browser OPEN, do the 2FA
yourself.

READ-ONLY: downloads statements and tax forms only. It never transfers
money, trades, rebalances, or changes any account setting.
Scope: statements + tax documents (1099s etc.). Tax forms contain your
SSN - handle accordingly.

Your files: this folder (Statements\, Tax Documents\).
Spouse's:  "Invest529 Receipts - spouse".
Full command reference: COMMANDS.txt in the parent folder.
